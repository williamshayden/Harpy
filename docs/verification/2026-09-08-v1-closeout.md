## Final waveform FFT addition

V1's feature scope closes with the optional `waveform-fft` classical baseline,
available through the CLI and public `waveform_fft_actor_spec()` factory. Its
[contract and study evidence](../v1-waveform-fft.md) retain the tested waveform
estimator, five-cent decoder and committed controller. Spectrum encoding,
qualified model weights and training behavior remain unchanged. Cell-max and
further model research are outside this v1 scope.

The direct production estimator was checked against **all 4,800 initial captures**
from the frozen spectrum-preservation study: 600 pairs under eight conditions.
Waveform and spectrum hashes, FFT estimates, first decisions and complete scheduled
plans matched exactly. Native Spectrum Peak also matched all 4,800 saved legacy
estimates and plans. Twenty supplemental source/control boundary comparisons
matched the frozen prototype. This is engineering equivalence to consumed study
evidence, not a new performance cohort or a rerun of every control trajectory.
The audit took 97.98 seconds on one CPU process without Torch/SB3; source and input
hashes remained unchanged.
